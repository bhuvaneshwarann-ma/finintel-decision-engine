HDFC BANK LIMITED - Q3 FY26 INVESTOR RESULTS & NET INTEREST INCOME UPDATE
Page 7: Profitability and Lending Growth Metrics
Net interest income (NII) grew by 15.4% YoY to INR 31,400 Crores with Net Interest Margin (NIM) stable at 3.65%.
