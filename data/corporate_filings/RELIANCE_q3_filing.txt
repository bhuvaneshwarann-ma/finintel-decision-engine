RELIANCE INDUSTRIES LIMITED - SEBI LODR REGULATORY DISCLOSURES Q3 FY26
Page 18: Capital Allocation, Debt Trajectory, and Balance Sheet Solvency
The consolidated net debt of the company remains well-controlled at INR 118,500 Crores against a tangible net worth exceeding INR 795,000 Crores.
The debt-to-equity ratio currently stands comfortably at 0.38x, reflecting prudent deleveraging across oil-to-chemicals (O2C) and retail divisions.
Jio Platforms subscriber base expanded to 488 million with ARPU rising to INR 198.5 driven by 5G tariff migration.
Retail footprint grew to 18,900 operational stores across consumer electronics, fashion, and grocery.
Capital expenditures in new energy gigafactories (solar PV, green hydrogen, and battery storage) are funded through strong operating cash flows of INR 38,200 Crores generated during the quarter.
Auditors confirmed zero statutory defaults or covenant breaches.
