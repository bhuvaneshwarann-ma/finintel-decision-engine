TATA CONSULTANCY SERVICES LIMITED - Q3 FY26 REVENUE UPDATE & OPERATIONAL HIGHLIGHTS
Page 4: Revenue, Operating Margins, and Order Inflow
Consolidated quarterly revenue grew by 8.4% YoY to INR 64,850 Crores.
Operating margin expanded by 50 bps sequentially to 26.0%, industry-leading among global IT consultancies.
Net profit after tax rose 8.2% YoY to INR 12,430 Crores.
Deal pipeline Total Contract Value (TCV) achieved USD 8.1 billion, driven by cloud modernization and enterprise AI transformational engagements.
Attrition in IT services moderated further to 11.5%.
Interim dividend of INR 10 per share and special dividend of INR 18 per share announced.
