XYZ CLEANPOWER & LOGISTICS INFRASTRUCTURE LIMITED - Q3 RESULTS UPDATE
Page 6: Financial Performance Summary
Operating revenue grew by 18.2% to INR 2,450 Crores driven by rapid execution of EPC solar projects.
However, net profit after tax declined by 12.0% YoY due to sharp increases in finance costs and high interest debt servicing.
Operating margins contracted to 4.5% compared to 8.2% in the prior year period.
Free cash flow from operations was negative INR 650 Crores, requiring external short-term commercial paper issuance.
Management acknowledges elevated leverage and plans asset monetization over an 18-month horizon.
