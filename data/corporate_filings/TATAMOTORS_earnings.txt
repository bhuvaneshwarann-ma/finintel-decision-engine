TATA MOTORS LIMITED - Q3 FY26 INVESTOR PRESENTATION & FINANCIAL SUMMARY
Page 4: Revenue and Profitability Metrics
Consolidated quarterly revenue grew 28.4% YoY to INR 110,577 Crores.
Consolidated EBITDA margin stood at 14.2%, an expansion of 190 basis points year-on-year.
Commercial Vehicle domestic market share expanded to 41.8% driven by infrastructure spending and fleet renewals.
Passenger EV market share maintained at 68.5% with the rollout of next-gen Acti.ev platforms.
Management reiterates the goal of achieving zero net automotive debt by Q1 FY27.
