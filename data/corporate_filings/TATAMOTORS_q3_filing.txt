TATA MOTORS LIMITED - DISCLOSURES UNDER SEBI LODR REGULATIONS Q3 FY26
Page 12: Capital Structure and Deleveraging Schedule
The consolidated net automotive debt reduced from INR 18,400 Crores to INR 6,200 Crores during the nine months ending December 31, 2025. 
The debt-to-equity ratio of the company currently stands improved at 0.62x compared to 1.15x in the previous fiscal year. 
Free cash flow generated at Jaguar Land Rover reached GBP 620 million in the quarter, driven by higher wholesale volumes of Range Rover and Defender models.
Promoter holding remains stable at 46.36% with zero shares pledged or encumbered. 
Capital expenditure guidance for electric mobility and battery architecture remains funded through internal operational accruals.
