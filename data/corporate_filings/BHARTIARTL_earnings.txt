BHARTI AIRTEL LIMITED - Q3 FY26 OPERATIONAL METRICS & FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS
Page 6: ARPU Growth, 5G Network Rollout, and Segment Profitability
Consolidated quarterly revenue grew by 16.4% YoY to INR 41,250 Crores.
India Mobile ARPU increased to INR 234 per month compared to INR 208 in the prior year, driven by premiumization and 4G/5G conversions.
Consolidated EBITDA margin stood at 53.2%, expanding 180 basis points YoY.
Net profit after tax grew 22.4% YoY to INR 3,850 Crores.
Airtel Business (B2B enterprise IoT and data center services) revenue expanded 14.8% YoY.
5G network coverage now spans over 7,500 cities and towns across India.
