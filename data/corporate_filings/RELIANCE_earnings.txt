RELIANCE INDUSTRIES LIMITED - Q3 FY26 FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE & REVENUE UPDATE
Page 5: Segment Performance and Profitability Summary
Consolidated quarterly revenue grew by 14.2% YoY to INR 248,500 Crores driven by strong consumer businesses growth.
Consolidated quarterly EBITDA increased to INR 44,600 Crores with an EBITDA margin of 17.9%.
Oil-to-Chemicals (O2C) segment maintained healthy refining margins despite global crude price volatility.
Jio recorded net profit growth of 16.5% YoY, benefiting from higher data consumption per user (32 GB/month).
Reliance Retail recorded revenue of INR 85,200 Crores, up 15.8% YoY with footfalls exceeding 280 million across India.
Management maintains full-year guidance for accelerated digital services monetization and robust green energy commercialization.
