INFOSYS LIMITED - Q3 FY26 OPERATIONAL PERFORMANCE AND REVENUE UPDATE
Page 3: Segmental Performance and Outlook
Constant currency revenue increased 9.2% YoY to INR 41,800 Crores.
Operating margin came in at 21.1%, reflecting tight cost optimization and utilization gains.
Large deal Total Contract Value (TCV) was signed at USD 3.2 billion, with 54% net new deals.
Attrition declined sequentially to 12.1%.
The company reaffirmed full-year constant currency revenue growth guidance of 8.5% to 10.0%.
Dividend payout commitment of over 75% of cumulative free cash flow over 5 years reiterated.
