ITC LIMITED - Q3 FY26 SEGMENT FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE & REVENUE UPDATE
Page 5: FMCG, Agribusiness, and Cigarettes Segment Results
Gross revenue from operations stood at INR 19,800 Crores, up 9.5% YoY driven by robust FMCG-Others growth.
Net profit after tax grew 11.2% YoY to INR 5,580 Crores.
FMCG-Others segment revenue reached INR 5,650 Crores, up 12.8% YoY with EBITDA margins expanding to 11.4%.
Agri-business revenue grew 14.5% supported by value-added agri exports and ITCMAARS digital platform expansion.
Paperboards and Specialty Papers segment revenue remained steady with focus on sustainable packaging solutions.
Declared interim dividend of INR 6.25 per share.
