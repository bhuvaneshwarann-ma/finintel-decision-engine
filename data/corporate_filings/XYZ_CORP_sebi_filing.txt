XYZ CLEANPOWER & LOGISTICS INFRASTRUCTURE LIMITED - SEBI LODR DISCLOSURES
Page 14: Note on Indebtedness, Promoter Pledges, and Going Concern Disclosures
The company reports a substantial surge in short-term and long-term borrowings, taking total outstanding consolidated debt to INR 14,850 Crores against a tangible net worth of INR 3,857 Crores.
The resulting debt-to-equity ratio has expanded to 3.85x, significantly breaching statutory covenants.
Promoter encumbrance and share pledge have reached 42.5% of total promoter holding as collateral for working capital credit lines.
Working capital interest coverage ratio has deteriorated to 1.18x, leaving minimal cushion against floating interest rate spikes.
Auditors have inserted an Emphasis of Matter regarding refinancing risk on INR 3,200 Crores of non-convertible debentures maturing within the next 6 months.
Off-balance-sheet corporate guarantees extended to unlisted subsidiaries stand at INR 2,100 Crores.
